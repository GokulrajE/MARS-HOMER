Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project, inspired by the user supplied underwater arcade references.

Final prompts:
1. Friendly orange/white right-facing clownfish, full body, expressive eye, dark outlines, painted cel shading, transparent background, no text/UI/other objects.
2. Wide 16:9 turquoise underwater background, overhead sunbeams, distant shipwreck at right, coral along bottom, open low-contrast central gameplay water, no character/obstacles/text/UI.
