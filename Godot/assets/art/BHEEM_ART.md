# Chhota Bheem: Laddoo Flight
The user supplied gemini_generated_video_b8e1cc2d.mp4 and a character asset reference. The video was sampled at two-second intervals to inspect framing, palette, flight movement, rewards and trail effects. This game is an interactive adaptation of that visual direction, not playback of the supplied video.

Artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool and stored in this folder:
- bheem_fly.png: Chhota Bheem, orange dhoti, red wrist bands, tilak, cheerful expression, riding a wooden broom facing right, warm painted cel shading, full isolated transparent sprite.
- bheem_world.png: wide Indian village panorama at golden sunset, terracotta temples, carved sandstone towers, thatched roofs, palms, river and distant hills, open upper sky, no characters or UI.
- bheem_pillar.png: isolated carved golden sandstone temple pillar, flat broad capital, floral reliefs, green vines, hand-painted sunset highlights, transparent background.

Animation is implemented in bheem_art.gd, bheem_fly.gdshader and bheem_sky.gdshader: eased banking, hover, straw flutter, reward lean, miss stars, magic trails, birds, kites, scrolling scenery and dusk lighting.
